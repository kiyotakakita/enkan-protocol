# Enkan Protocol (円環プロトコル)

> **A Backflow Royalty & Cultural Commons Protocol**  
> Turning NFTs from Speculative Commodities into Generative Cultural Resources.

## 📖 Philosophy

### 1. NFT as a Resource, Not a Speculative Good
Conventional NFTs failed by artificially imposing scarcity and relying on market supply/demand curves. In genuine culture, masterworks are abundant natural resources (like a Waterfall or Water Source).

### 2. "Every Manga Artist Starts by Tracing"
No creator begins in a vacuum. A novice manga artist inevitably traces, copies, and breaks down the master's lines into their own muscle memory. Copying is the legitimate physical training of cultural inheritance.

### 3. Escaping the "Street Performer" Economy
In current social platforms, amateur creators perform daily on the digital street, hoping for established influencers to toss an "alms" (a brief quote-retweet acting as cultural welfare). Enkan Protocol breaks this asymmetric dependency.

### 4. Backflow Circulation (円環 / The Slow Web 🌐)
- **Free to Fork & Trace:** Anyone can fork and trace upstream works at zero licensing cost during their apprenticeship.
- **Backflow Royalty:** When the successor eventually succeeds, revenue automatically flows *back upstream* to the original master via smart contracts.
